A quiet morning began beside the river. The water carried leaves past the old bridge, and a small boat waited near a wooden dock. A woman walked along the path with a notebook in her hand. She stopped to watch a bird leave a branch and fly over the water. Under the bridge, the air was cool and smelled of stone and rain.

Across the river, people opened the windows of their houses. A baker put fresh bread on a shelf and swept flour away from the front door. A young boy rode a bicycle through the square, looking for his friends. The friends were already playing a game near the fountain. They called to the boy, who crossed the square and joined their game.

Before noon, clouds gathered above the hills. Light rain fell on the roofs and left dark marks on the pavement. The woman closed her notebook and walked into a shop. There were maps on the wall, cups on a table, and a warm lamp beside the window. She sat by the lamp with tea and wrote about the river, the bird, and the morning.
